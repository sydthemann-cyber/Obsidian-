###### Abstract
### Summary

- Core genomic islands (**VPI-2, VSP-I, VSP-II**) function primarily as **defense islands**, protecting 7PET *V. cholerae* against phages and plasmids rather than directly contributing to virulence.
- Conserved defense systems include **DdmABC/DdmDE**, **CBASS**, **T1RM**, **TgvAB**, **AvcD**, and additional lineage-specific systems encoded on VSP-II variants.
- Many defense systems are encoded on **mobile genetic elements (MGEs)**, linking bacterial immunity with horizontal gene transfer.
- Several defense systems remain poorly characterized because many natural *V. cholerae* phages are unavailable for laboratory study.
- The chromosomal **integron** functions as a reservoir ("biobank") of defense genes that can diversify bacterial immunity.
- Pandemic success likely depends not only on virulence factors but also on the acquisition and maintenance of effective phage defense systems. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Functions

- enables CTXΦ infection through TCP receptor
