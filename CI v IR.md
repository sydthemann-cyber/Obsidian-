|                     | Cumulative incidence                                                        | Incidence rate                                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Units               | None (proportion)                                                           | 1 / person-time                                                                   |
| Range               | 0–1                                                                         | 0 to ∞                                                                            |
| Directly calculable | Closed population followed over time                                        | Closed or open population with individual time-at-risk data                       |
| Indirectly          | Survival methods with censoring (K-M)                                       | Person-time ≈ average population at risk × observation length (large pop, low IR) |
| Limitations         | Ignores when the outcome occurred; loss to follow-up makes outcomes unknown | Assumes rate constant across person-time                                          |
| Aliases             | Risk, attack rate, incidence proportion                                     | Person-time incidence rate, hazard rate                                           |
