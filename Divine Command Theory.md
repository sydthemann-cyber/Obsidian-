an act is right **if and only if** God commands it.

problems: 
- it is only right **because** it is commanded 
	- in that cause then something being go is arbitrary, god could have easily commanded something else be right 
	- if right = commanded by god, then something is commanded by god if it is commanded by god 
- it is only commanded because it is right
	- then what is good and bad is a priori to god, so how does god determine if something is right?
- 'believers and nonbelievers inhabit the same moral universe' --> they do not know how to decide if something is moral 

Practical problems: atheists won’t accept it; different faiths conflict with no way to resolve it; it can’t ground public ethics in a pluralist society.
