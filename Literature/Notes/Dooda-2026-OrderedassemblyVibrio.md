###### Abstract

### Summary

- systematically resolved the biosynthetic pathway responsible for Vibrio polysaccharide (VPS) production
- demonstrated that VPS is synthesized through an ordered Wzx/Wzy-dependent pathway
- identified lipid-linked intermediates using LC-MS coupled with targeted vps deletion mutants
- assigned biochemical functions to nearly every Vps biosynthetic enzyme
- showed that VpsD generates both major and minor VPS variants through substrate flexibility
- establishes the first complete mechanistic model of VPS repeat-unit assembly

### Detailed Notes

#### VPS structure

VPS consists of two closely related polysaccharides.

##### Major variant (~80%)

Repeat unit contains

- α-D-Gal
- β-D-Glc
- α-D-Glc
- α-L-GulNAcAGly3OAc

---

##### Minor variant

Differs only by

- α-D-GlcNAc replacing α-D-Glc

---

#### Overall biosynthetic pathway

VPS is synthesized through a canonical **Wzx/Wzy-dependent pathway**.

Overall order

1. initiation
2. repeat-unit assembly
3. repeat-unit modification
4. membrane flipping
5. polymerization
6. export

Assembly occurs on **bactoprenyl phosphate (Und-P)**.

---

#### VpsL

##### Function

Initiating phosphoglycosyltransferase (PGT)

Transfers

- glucose
- UDP-glucose
- bactoprenyl phosphate

Produces

- Und-PP-Glc

First committed step of VPS biosynthesis.

---

#### VpsA-VpsB-VpsK module

Functions together to generate and transfer the unusual sugar precursor.

Produces

- ManNAcA-derived residue

Strong homology to

- Enterobacterial Common Antigen (ECA) biosynthesis

---

#### VpsJ

##### Function

Previously unknown enzyme.

Acts as

- C5 epimerase

Converts

- ManNAcA
- ↓
- L-GulNAcA

Produces the rare sugar found within mature VPS.

---

#### VpsH

##### Function

Novel modifying enzyme

Introduces

- glycine modification

Modification is

- not required for repeat-unit assembly
- influences mature matrix properties

---

#### VpsG

##### Function

Acetyltransferase

Adds

- acetyl modification

Like VpsH

- dispensable for assembly
- contributes to mature biofilm matrix

---

#### VpsI

##### Function

Glycosyltransferase

Adds one of the final sugars during repeat-unit assembly.

Acts after VpsH/G modifications.

---

#### VpsD

##### Function

Final glycosyltransferase

Completes repeat-unit assembly.

Displays substrate flexibility.

Produces

- major VPS
- minor VPS

Single enzyme responsible for both polysaccharide variants.

---

#### VpsE

##### Function

Predicted Wzx flippase

Moves completed repeat unit

cytoplasm

↓

periplasm

Acts only after repeat-unit completion.

---

#### VpsF

##### Function

Predicted Wzy polymerase

Polymerizes flipped repeat units into mature VPS.

Acts downstream of VpsE.

---

#### Lipid-linked intermediates

LC-MS detected sequential accumulation of intermediates following deletion of specific enzymes.

Allowed direct assignment of enzyme order.

Demonstrates

- ordered biosynthesis
- minimal branching
- linear repeat-unit assembly

---

#### Major conclusions

- VPS biosynthesis is highly ordered
- nearly every Vps enzyme now has an assigned biochemical role
- VpsD generates VPS structural diversity
- VpsH and VpsG modify mature polysaccharide rather than assemble it
- lipid-linked intermediate profiling is an effective approach for resolving polysaccharide biosynthesis
