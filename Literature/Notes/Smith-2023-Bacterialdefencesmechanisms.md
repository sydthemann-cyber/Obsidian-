### Summary

- Bacterial defenses evolved in response to **competitor bacteria, phages, and predators**, and many now also confer antimicrobial resistance.
- Defenses operate at **molecular, cellular, and multicellular** scales.
- Most defenses either **prevent attack, neutralize harmful agents, repair damage, or coordinate collective protection**.
- Defense deployment is regulated by **competition sensing, danger sensing, stress responses, and quorum sensing**.
- Horizontal gene transfer is a major driver of defense evolution, spreading resistance genes and anti-phage systems.
- Attackers continuously evolve counter-defenses, producing an evolutionary arms race that shapes bacterial diversity and antimicrobial resistance. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
