### Summary
The **7PET lineage** is the seventh-pandemic _Vibrio cholerae_ lineage that arose from an O1 El Tor ancestor and became globally successful because of a combination of virulence, transmission fitness, and strong mobile-element-associated defense systems. Across the notes, a major theme is that 7PET is not defined only by toxins and colonization factors, but also by a genomic “immune system” on its core islands—especially **VPI-2**, **VSP-I**, and **VSP-II**—that protects against phages and plasmids. Over time, different 7PET sublineages evolved distinct defense repertoires, and these shifts likely shaped epidemiology, lineage replacement, and global spread.

### Detailed notes
- The **seventh pandemic El Tor lineage (7PET)** is the dominant pandemic lineage of _Vibrio cholerae_.
- It emerged as a highly successful clone with:
    - cholera toxin production
    - TCP-mediated colonization
    - strong mobile genetic element acquisition
    - lineage-specific defense systems
- 7PET evolved through repeated waves and regional diversification rather than as a single static lineage.
