#### Ecological selection

Livestock environments strongly select for antimicrobial resistance through

- antimicrobial exposure
- dense host populations
- increased transmission
- accessory gene acquisition

Agricultural systems function as important reservoirs of adaptive resistance genes.

#### Healthy microbiome reservoir

Healthy individuals contain persistent plasmids carrying antimicrobial resistance genes.

These plasmids

- circulate without disease
- transfer across bacterial genera
- may disseminate ARGs into opportunistic pathogens

Examples

- erm(B)
- aphA
- cfr(C)
