### summary
bacteriocins are diffusible, peptide-based toxins produced by bacteria to inhibit or kill closely related species or competitors. they function as antibacterial weapons, contributing to ecological competition and microbial community structure.

### Related
- [[Endotoxins]]

---

#### mechanisms of action
- form pores in competitor cell membranes  
- degrade cell walls through peptidoglycan hydrolysis  
- damage nucleic acids (DNA or RNA)  
- some employ immunity proteins to protect producing cells  

---

#### biological significance
- mediate inter-bacterial competition within mixed communities  
- help establish niche dominance and prevent invasion by non-kin strains  
- may serve as templates for novel antimicrobial development
