### Summary
- **CTXΦ** is the cholera toxin-encoding filamentous phage carried by pathogenic _Vibrio cholerae_.
- It is a key virulence determinant in pandemic cholera strains, including 7PET lineages and O139.
- In the context of the previous notes, CTXΦ is important not only because it encodes toxin, but because it sits inside a larger evolutionary system involving:
    - host colonization
    - serogroup switching
    - lineage-specific defense systems
    - phage–host coevolution
    - pandemic spread and lineage replacement
