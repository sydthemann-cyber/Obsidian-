### Summary
- Chitinases are extracellular glycosyl hydrolases that degrade chitin into GlcNAc oligomers/monomers
- Support nutrient acquisition, chitin colonization, competence & biofilm dynamics in *V. cholerae*

#### Chitin degradation
- Chitin (β-1,4 GlcNAc) → GlcNAc oligomers → GlcNAc
- Products used as C & N source
- Chitin oligos induce natural competence
#### Genes
-  chiA-1: major extracellular chitinase; insoluble/surface chitin degradation
- chiA-2: secondary chitinase; soluble chitin hydrolysis
- Expression induced by chitin & regulated by cell density
#### Functions
- Nutrient acquisition
- Chitin surface colonization
- HGT via competence induction
- Co-expressed with dns & xds during growth on chitin
- Biofilm remodeling
#### Properties
- Enzyme: extracellular glycosyl hydrolase
- Substrate: chitin (β-1,4 GlcNAc)
- Products: GlcNAc + GlcNAc oligomers
- Localization: extracellular/periplasmic
