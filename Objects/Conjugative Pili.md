# Conjugative Pili

### Summary

Conjugative pili are extracellular appendages assembled by Type IV secretion systems (T4SS) that mediate plasmid transfer during bacterial conjugation. Many plasmid-dependent phages exploit conjugative pili as receptors, creating an evolutionary trade-off between horizontal gene transfer and phage susceptibility.

### Notes

- encoded by conjugative plasmids
- assembled by T4SS
- establish donor-recipient contact
- retract during DNA transfer
- receptor for plasmid-dependent phages
- maintenance favored when conjugation is beneficial
- loss prevents horizontal gene transfer
- common target of adaptive mutations during phage selection
