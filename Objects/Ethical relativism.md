there is no universal moral thesis (you cannot get an ought from an is) 

diversity thesis:
- moral beliefs vary across cultures
dependency thesis: 
- moral standards validity depends on acceptance by the culture 

problems:
- tolerance would be relative
	- the diversity thesis is supposed to promote tolerance, but if someones culture does not believe tolerance is moral, then they do not have to do so 
	- cannot criticize things we think are immoral that are done by another group 
	- reformers would always be wrong - since they are trying to change something that the group currently considers morally permissible 
		- there would be stagnation, since what is moral is defined by the present moment
	- trying to conclude an ought from an is

other questions:
- what defines a group or culture? 
- what if you belong to more than one group
