### summary
extracellular dna (edna) serves as both a structural and functional component of Biofilms. it stabilizes the matrix, promotes adhesion, and provides a nutrient and phosphate source for v. cholerae and other bacteria.

#### functions
- mediates initial surface attachment  
- reinforces Biofilm structural integrity  
- serves as a nutrient source under phosphate limitation  
- interacts with vps and eps to form a cohesive extracellular matrix  

---

#### regulation + turnover
- degraded by extracellular nucleases (Dns, Xds)  
  - deletion of these enzymes enhances biofilm formation and impairs detachment  
- degradation produces nucleoside monophosphates, which are taken up via **NupC** proteins  
- sole phosphate source for v. cholerae under specific growth conditions
