### Summary

Genome-wide association studies (GWAS) identify genetic variants statistically associated with specific phenotypes across bacterial populations.

#### Related

- [[Pangenome]]
- [[Comparative Genomics]]

---

### Detailed Notes

#### Bacterial GWAS

Associates genetic variation with traits such as

- host species
- antimicrobial resistance
- metabolism
- virulence
- ecological niche

---

#### Common inputs

- SNPs
- accessory genes
- unitig k-mers
- gene presence/absence

---

#### Applications

- identify adaptive loci
- identify host-associated genes
- predict ecological specialization
- investigate bacterial evolution

---

#### Limitations

- population structure can produce false associations
- requires correction for phylogeny
- identifies associations rather than causation
