### Related
- [[Mobile Genetic Elements]]

##### Ecological drivers

- microbial co-occurrence independently predicts HGT after controlling for phylogeny
- repeated ecological encounters increase opportunities for:
  - conjugation
  - transformation
  - transduction
- shared environments promote retention of adaptive transferred genes
- particle-associated communities are important HGT hotspots

##### Ecological adaptation

Accessory genes acquired through HGT contribute to adaptation across ecological niches.

Frequently affected traits include

- metabolism
- nutrient acquisition
- antimicrobial resistance
- host colonization

HGT allows rapid adaptation without divergence of the core genome.

#### Broad-host-range plasmids

Broad-host-range plasmids facilitate HGT across distantly related bacteria.

Characteristics

- conserved replication machinery
- repeated cross-species transfer
- modular acquisition of adaptive genes

Healthy microbiomes can function as reservoirs supporting future dissemination into pathogens.
