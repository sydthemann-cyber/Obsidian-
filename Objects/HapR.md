### summary
hapR is the high-cell-density quorum sensing transcriptional regulator in v. cholerae. it represses Biofilm formation and virulence factor expression, acting antagonistically to aphA and vpsT.

### functions
- negatively regulates Biofilm formation  
- represses transcription of vpsL, vpsT, and c-di-gmp-metabolizing enzymes  
- integrates signals from quorum sensing networks to trigger dispersal and downregulate virulence
