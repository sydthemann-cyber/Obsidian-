#### Alternative lysogenic outcomes

Classical lysogeny is not the only high-MOI outcome.

Alternative fate:

- λ remains as a non-replicating episome (pλ)
- expresses CI
- does not integrate
- segregates asymmetrically
- produces transiently immune daughter cells

This allows continued lytic transmission while maintaining lysogenic immunity.
