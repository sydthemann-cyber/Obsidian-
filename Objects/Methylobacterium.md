Methylobacterium species constitute dominant members of the leaf microbiome (40), play critical ecological roles from affecting plant health to influencing biogeo chemical cycles (41–43), and constitute model systems for a number of biotechnology applications (44–46). Temperature variation is a major abiotic factor shaping microbial communities associated with plants (47, 48). Methylobacterium communities isolated from leaf surfaces display distinct ranges of optimum growth temperature, suggesting temperature as a critical factor shaping community diversity and composition
