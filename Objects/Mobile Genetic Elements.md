#### Ecology of MGE transfer

- MGE transmission depends on ecological overlap between hosts
- repeated co-occurrence increases opportunities for plasmid, phage, and extracellular DNA transfer
- particle-associated communities facilitate MGE dissemination

#### Modular evolution

Some MGEs possess

- conserved maintenance backbone
- highly variable cargo region

This architecture allows stable inheritance while rapidly exchanging adaptive genes such as antimicrobial resistance determinants.
