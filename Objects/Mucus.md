#### Mucus as a biofilm scaffold

Intestinal mucus functions as both a nutrient source and a structural matrix.

Host mucus

- contains diverse glycans
- creates physical confinement
- supports embedded microcolony formation
- mechanically constrains colony expansion

Rather than forming surface-attached biofilms, bacteria can grow within mucus-lined cavities, producing host-associated biofilm architectures distinct from abiotic biofilms.
