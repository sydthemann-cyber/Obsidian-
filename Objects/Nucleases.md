### summary
dns is an extracellular endonuclease produced by Vibrio cholerae and other bacteria. it degrades edna within Biofilms and host tissues, contributing to nutrient acquisition, Biofilm dispersal, and virulence. dns activity is regulated by environmental cues and the vpsT–hapRnetwork.

### functional roles
- **biofilm dispersal** → hydrolyzes edna to release cells from mature biofilms  
- **virulence** → degrades host neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs)  
- **nutrient recycling** → liberates nucleotides from extracellular DNA  
- **regulation** → upregulated in low-nutrient or dispersal conditions
