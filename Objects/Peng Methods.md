The chitin particles were then inoculated with _V. cholerae_ under stationary conditions for 30 minutes to allow cells to colonize the chitin surface. After this inoculation, flow was resumed at 0.1 µL/min, corresponding to an average flow velocity of 14 µm/s, which is comparable to the sinking speed of marine snow particles ([59](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-59)–[61](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-61)). All strains of _V. cholerae_ were derived from E7946, a pathogenic isolate of the El Tor Ogawa lineage ([62](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-62)). All temperate phage strains were derived from _V. cholerae_ bacteriophage K139 from the kappa family of O1 phages ([63](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-63), [64](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-64)). As is expected to be the norm among temperate phages with repressor-mediated lysogeny, K139 lysogens exhibit superinfection immunity: phages can adsorb to and inject their genome into lysogens, but this does not lead to lytic infection ([64](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-64)). Lysogens can therefore act as sinks for unproductive adsorption of closely related phages. To distinguish between parental lysogen and parental non-lysogen (i.e., phage susceptible) subpopulations, we used constitutive _mKate22_ and _mKO-κ_ fluorescent protein constructs integrated in a neutral site in the _V. cholerae_ genome. Control experiments indicated that K139 lysogens and non-lysogens have indistinguishable biofilm growth dynamics when they are inoculated independently in monoculture ([SI Appendix](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#app-1), [Fig. S2](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#F5)); additionally, the _mKate22_ and _mKO-κ_ fluorescent protein constructs do not influence competition outcomes in co-culture ([SI Appendix](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#app-1), [Fig. S3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#F6)). Data were acquired by confocal microscopy of the chitin-attached _V. cholerae_ biofilms and analyzed using the image processing framework BiofilmQ ([65](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.26.690769v2.full#ref-65)).
