#### Evolutionary steering through receptor loss

- T4P-targeting phages select for mutations that reduce pilus function
- resulting strains may have reduced
  - twitching motility
  - attachment
  - biofilm development
  - virulence

#### Resistance stability

- not all resistance mutations are equally stable
- point mutations, deletions, and nonsense mutations remained resistant after phage removal
- tandem duplications could revert rapidly
- reversible resistance may allow restoration of virulence after treatment ends

#### Treatment implications

- phage cocktails should target multiple receptors
- cocktail design should account for
  - resistance mechanism
  - cross-resistance
  - mutation reversibility
  - fitness and virulence consequences
