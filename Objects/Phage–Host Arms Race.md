#### Infection heterogeneity

Temperate phages generate heterogeneous host populations through non-integrated prophage states.

Benefits include:

- prolonged host availability
- continued horizontal transmission
- reduced depletion of susceptible hosts
- increased persistence during host scarcity

#### Receptor-dependent resistance

- PO4 selection produced mutations across 16 T4P-associated genes
- resistance arose through disruption of
  - pilus assembly
  - pilin processing
  - pilin regulation
  - pilus retraction
- most mutations blocked several T4P-targeting phages simultaneously
- receptor-targeting phages can select broad cross-resistance when they depend on shared machinery

#### Evolutionary reversibility

- tandem duplications may provide temporary resistance
- removal of phage pressure can select reversion to receptor function
- mutation architecture influences whether resistance becomes stable or reversible
