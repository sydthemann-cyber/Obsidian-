#### Stealth plasmids

Highly prevalent plasmids can persist within healthy microbiomes while remaining largely undetected by conventional metagenomic analyses.

Characteristics

- highly conserved replication backbone
- modular accessory region
- broad host range
- long-term persistence

Example

- pGut1
