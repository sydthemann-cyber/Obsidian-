### Summary

The prophage-like (pλ) state is a non-integrated, non-replicating episomal state adopted by bacteriophage λ during high-MOI infections.

#### Related

- [[Temperate Phage]]
- [[Lysogeny]]
- [[Transient Immunity]]

---

### Detailed Notes

#### Characteristics

- episomal
- non-replicating
- CI expression maintained
- asymmetric segregation
- no chromosomal integration

---

#### Consequences

- produces transiently immune λ-free siblings
- extends horizontal transmission
- increases infection heterogeneity
- supports long-term phage-host coexistence

---

#### Distinction from lysogeny

| Lysogen | pλ |
|---------|----|
| integrated | episomal |
| replicated with chromosome | non-replicating |
| permanent inheritance | asymmetric inheritance |
| stable immunity | transient immunity in some progeny |
