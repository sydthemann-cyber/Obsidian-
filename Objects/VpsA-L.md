# VPS Genes

### Summary

The VPS genes encode the enzymes and regulators responsible for synthesis, modification, export, and transcriptional regulation of Vibrio polysaccharide.

---

#### VpsL

Initiating phosphoglycosyltransferase (PGT).

Catalyzes

UDP-Glucose + Und-P

↓

Und-PP-Glc

Represents the first committed step of VPS biosynthesis.
### VpsR

- master transcriptional activator
- activates VPS operons
- responds to c-di-GMP

### VpsT

- c-di-GMP-binding transcription factor
- cooperates with VpsR
- activates VPS expression

---

### VpsD

- glycosyltransferase
- repeat-unit assembly

### VpsG

- terminal glycosyltransferase
- final sugar addition

---


### VpsH

- acetyltransferase
- acetylates VPS repeat units
- contributes to mature matrix properties
- generates major and minor VPS repeat units

---
