= new [[incident cases]] / population at risk 

CI = # incident cases / # initially at risk (over a time period)

**CI from IR (rare outcome, large pop)**`CI ≈ IR × t`

`CI = 1 − e^(−IR × t)`Accounts for the shrinking at-risk pool. Diverges from IR×t when outcome is common.

Also called _risk, attack rate, incidence proportion_. Probability that a person develops the outcome over a specified period. Unitless, 0–1.

eg: 2% cumulative incidence of disease X over 2 years (if 200 new cases out of 10,000 at risk)
