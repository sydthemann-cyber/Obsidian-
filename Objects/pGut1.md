### Summary

pGut1 is a highly prevalent broad-host-range plasmid identified within healthy human gut microbiomes that combines a conserved maintenance backbone with an extremely flexible accessory region.

### Detailed Notes

#### Backbone

Approximately 4 kb.

Encodes

- replication protein
- recombinase
- YafQ-RelB toxin-antitoxin system

Highly conserved among hosts.

---

#### Variable region

- 40 identified insertion variants
- modular "plug-and-play" architecture
- extensive structural diversity

Frequently carries

- erm(B)
- aphA
- cfr(C)
- tet(40)

---

#### Host range

Identified across

- 49 genera
- 53 species
- 2 bacterial phyla

Includes

- Clostridioides difficile
- Salmonella enterica

---

#### Biological significance

- broad-host-range plasmid
- persistent in healthy microbiomes
- disseminates antimicrobial resistance
- reservoir for future pathogen evolution
