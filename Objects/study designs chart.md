|Category|Design|Measures|Main strengths|Main limitations|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Descriptive|Case report / case series|Counts, descriptions|Hypothesis generation|No comparison group|
|Population surveillance|Prevalence, incidence trends|Detect epidemics|Not causal|
|Analytic, observational|Cross-sectional ("snapshot")|Prevalence, prevalence ratio, means, correlation|Fast, cheap|Temporality unclear; prevalent cases over-represent chronic disease; no incidence|
|Cohort ("film"), prospective or retrospective|Incidence, RR, IRR, RD, change over time|Absolute risks; multiple outcomes; exposure measured before disease; can oversample rare exposures|Costly and slow (prospective); loss to follow-up; poor for rare outcomes|
|Case-control|Odds ratio|Efficient for rare diseases; quick, cheap|No absolute risk; OR ≈ RR only if rare; selection bias; recall bias|
|Ecological|Group-level correlations|Hypothesis generation|Ecologic fallacy; high confounding|
|Analytic, experimental|Randomized controlled trial|RR, RD, etc.|Controls measured and unmeasured confounders; precise exposure|Expensive; external validity; dropouts, crossovers, unblinding; ethics; rare outcomes impractical|
