## Presentation notes
#### Anatomy: 
Drill (females have a larger one) – drill all the way in to lay their eggs 
Two layers (softer inside, mouth) and a small jaw at the end of the rostrum 
Scratch to make grooves and then they have the head that can let them swivel back and forth and drill around 
Antennae allow them to sense an acorn (olfactory receptors/pheromone sensing) 
EYES: compound eyes, wide view and motion detection (near sighted) 
Elytra: could fly but it’s not the most elegant (do they climb up the tree???) – they don’t rely on flying 
Hardwings: defense purposes 
Ovipositer: on their back – can smell and taste for quality 
Sensory: 
Olfactory sensilla with receptor neurons for VOCs and pheromones (Unclear on this, Hostmarking) 
Ovipositors: can sense the texture and chemoreceptors 
#### Life Cycle: 
34 eggs 
Eggs laid and dropped prematurely 
Larvae will leave and bury into the ground 
2 years buried as larvae and they always emerge in the spring 
They only live for a few weeks to months
