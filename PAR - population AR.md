amount of disease incidence in the **total population** that is due to the exposure. It measures the exposure's contribution to the overall incidence in the population.

PAR = It− I₀

PAR = AR × pₜ = (Iₑ − I₀) × pₜ

- I_T = incidence in the total population (exposed and unexposed combined)
- I₀ = incidence in the unexposed
- pₜ = proportion of the total population that is exposed
