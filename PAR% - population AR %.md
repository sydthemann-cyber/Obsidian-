PAR% is the percent of **all cases in the population** that are caused by the exposure.

PAR% = (I_T − I₀) / I_T × 100 = PAR / I_T × 100

PAR% = AR% × p_c

PAR% = [(RR − 1) / RR] × p_c × 100

- I_T = incidence in the total population
- I₀ = incidence in the unexposed
- **p_c = proportion of cases who are exposed.** Note that this is not pₜ, the proportion of the population exposed, which is what the PAR formula uses.
