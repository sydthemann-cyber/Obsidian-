# Plasmid-dependent Phages

### Summary

Plasmid-dependent phages (PDPs) infect bacteria by recognizing conjugative pili encoded by plasmids. They simultaneously kill plasmid-bearing bacteria and select against plasmid mobility, making them promising tools for limiting antimicrobial resistance dissemination.

### Notes

- receptor = conjugative pilus
- broad host range
- target conjugative plasmids
- reduce horizontal gene transfer
- select for non-conjugative plasmids
- impose trade-off between conjugation and phage resistance
- candidate therapy against MDR plasmids
