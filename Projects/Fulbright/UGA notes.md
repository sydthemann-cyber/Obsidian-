#### Osterman group
- "The Osterman Group investigates bacterial defense and phage counter-defense strategies to discover new enzymes, metabolites, and pathways linking metabolism and immunity across the tree of life."
- "In recent years, our understanding of bacterial immunity against bacteriophages has expanded dramatically. One prominent class of anti-phage defense systems operates by depleting essential metabolites such as nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD⁺), leading to growth arrest or cell death. Recent work has shown that phages can overcome these defenses by rebuilding NAD⁺ using previously unknown enzymes and biochemical reactions, as well as by producing novel small molecules."

##### Chemistry biology and health

- chronic disease, cancer , structural and integrative cellular biology 
	- "Université Grenoble Alpes conducts fundamental preclinical and clinical research, particularly in the fields of chronic diseases, rare diseases and cancer. It relies on interdisciplinary approaches and on the construction of a continuum of scientific expertise from the molecule to the population, covering genetics, epigenetics, cell biology, cell-matrix interactions, immunology, metabolism, the study of host-pathogen interactions, and environmental epidemiology.
