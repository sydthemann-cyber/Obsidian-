risk difference vs risk ratio (choosing)
   * RR → **strength** of association
   * RD → potential **public health impact**
RD / RR interpretation
   * RD = 0 / RR = 1 → no association
   * RD > 0 / RR > 1 → positive association
   * RD < 0 / RR < 1 → negative association
