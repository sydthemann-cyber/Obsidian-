### Summary

Stealth plasmids are highly prevalent plasmids that persist within microbial communities but remain poorly represented in existing plasmid databases.

#### Related

- [[Plasmid]]
- [[Antimicrobial Resistance]]

---

### Detailed Notes

#### Characteristics

- broad host range
- long-term persistence
- highly conserved backbone
- modular accessory region
- frequently overlooked by metagenomic analyses

---

#### Backbone

Encodes

- replication proteins
- recombination proteins
- stability systems
- toxin-antitoxin modules

---

#### Variable region

Contains rapidly changing cargo

Examples

- ARGs
- accessory genes
- hypothetical proteins

---

#### Biological roles

- long-term persistence
- dissemination of adaptive genes
- cross-species HGT
- maintenance of cryptic resistomes
