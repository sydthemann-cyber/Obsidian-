#### Non-integrated prophage states

- temperate phages may enter a non-integrated **prophage-like (pλ)** state instead of stable lysogeny
- pλ remains episomal while expressing CI-mediated immunity
- pλ segregates asymmetrically during cell division
- generates transiently immune daughter cells lacking phage DNA
- extends opportunities for horizontal transmission during host scarcity
