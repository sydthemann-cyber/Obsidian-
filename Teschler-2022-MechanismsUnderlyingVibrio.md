### Summary

- Biofilm formation is a regulated developmental process: **attachment → maturation → dispersal**.
- **c-di-GMP** is the master regulator controlling the transition between motile and sessile lifestyles.
- Biofilm architecture depends on coordinated production of **VPS, matrix proteins, eDNA, and OMVs**.
- Environmental signals are integrated through **c-di-GMP, transcription factors, sRNAs, and two-component systems**.
- **Quorum sensing (HapR)** represses matrix production and promotes dispersal at high cell density.
- Biofilm dispersal is an active developmental process.
- Spatial organization generates emergent properties including stress tolerance, cooperation, and phage protection. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

#### Surface Attachment

##### Flagellum

- mediates swimming
- promotes surface sensing
- involved in initial attachment
- motility decreases following attachment

##### MSHA pili

- primary attachment pili
- reversible attachment
- surface exploration
- promoted by high c-di-GMP

##### Adhesins

- **FrhA**
- **CraA**
- **Bap1**
- stabilize irreversible attachment

---

#### c-di-GMP

##### Functions

High c-di-GMP

- ↑ VPS synthesis
- ↑ matrix protein production
- ↑ attachment
- ↓ motility
- ↓ dispersal

Low c-di-GMP

- ↑ motility
- ↑ dispersal
- ↓ matrix production

##### Regulation

Produced by

- diguanylate cyclases (DGCs)

Degraded by

- phosphodiesterases (PDEs)

Acts through

- transcription factors
- riboswitches
- protein effectors

Functions

- structural scaffold
- biofilm cohesion
- mechanical stability
- protection

Genes

- **vps-I**
- **vps-II**

##### Matrix Proteins

**RbmA**

- cell-cell adhesion
- microcolony formation
- structural integrity

**RbmC**

- biofilm stability
- matrix organization

**Bap1**

- surface anchoring
- biofilm expansion

##### Other Matrix Components

**eDNA**

- structural reinforcement
- interacts with VPS

**OMVs**

- transport
- signaling
- enzyme delivery
- matrix organization

---

#### Biofilm Regulation

##### Positive Regulators

**VpsR**

- activates VPS synthesis
- activated by c-di-GMP

**VpsT**

- c-di-GMP-responsive regulator
- activates matrix genes

##### Negative Regulators

**HapR**

- represses VPS genes
- represses matrix proteins
- promotes dispersal
- activated at high cell density

**H-NS**

- global transcriptional repressor
- represses biofilm-associated genes

**ToxR**

- integrates environmental signals
- regulates virulence and biofilm formation

---

#### Small Regulatory RNAs

##### VadR

- activated by VxrAB
- represses **vpsU**
- represses **vpsL**
- represses **rbmA**
- inhibits mature biofilm formation

##### VrrA

- regulated by RpoE
- represses **rbmC**
- regulates OMV production
- links envelope stress to biofilm regulation

#### Quorum Sensing

Low cell density

- biofilm formation favored

High cell density

- HapR activation
- matrix repression
- dispersal promoted
