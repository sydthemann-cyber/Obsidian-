### Summary

Transient immunity is a temporary phage-resistant state produced by inheritance of phage repressors without inheritance of the phage genome.

#### Related

- [[Temperate Phage]]
- [[Lysogeny]]
- [[Phage Life Cycle]]

---

### Detailed Notes

#### Mechanism

- daughter cell inherits CI protein
- phage chromosome is absent
- CI represses incoming phage gene expression
- immunity is gradually lost through protein dilution

---

#### Biological roles

- delays reinfection
- generates heterogeneous populations
- prolongs phage-host coexistence
- maintains susceptible hosts within populations
