# VPS

### Summary

**Vibrio polysaccharide (VPS)** is the primary exopolysaccharide of the *Vibrio cholerae* biofilm matrix. It forms the structural scaffold that anchors cells together, promotes surface attachment, and provides mechanical stability to mature biofilms. VPS production is tightly regulated by intracellular **c-di-GMP** levels and environmental signals through the transcriptional regulators **VpsR** and **VpsT**, while synthesis, modification, and export are carried out by the VPS gene products.

### Detailed Notes

#### Composition

- major exopolysaccharide component of the *V. cholerae* extracellular matrix
- composed of repeating carbohydrate units synthesized by the VPS biosynthetic machinery
- exists as both major and minor repeat-unit variants
- repeat units can be acetylated, altering polymer properties

---

#### Biological functions

- forms the structural scaffold of mature biofilms
- promotes irreversible surface attachment
- mediates cell-cell cohesion
- stabilizes three-dimensional biofilm architecture
- anchors matrix proteins (e.g., RbmA, RbmC, Bap1)
- protects cells from
  - mechanical disruption
  - environmental stress
  - antimicrobial compounds
  - predation

---

#### Regulation

VPS production is primarily controlled by intracellular **c-di-GMP**.

High c-di-GMP

↓

activation of **VpsR** and **VpsT**

↓

transcription of VPS biosynthetic operons

↓

VPS synthesis

↓

biofilm maturation

VPS production is also integrated with

- quorum sensing
- nutrient availability
- environmental conditions
- surface sensing

---

#### Biosynthesis

- synthesized by proteins encoded within the VPS operons
- repeat units assembled by glycosyltransferases
- repeat units modified by acetyltransferases
- mature polymer exported to the cell surface
- detailed biosynthetic enzymes summarized in genes

---

#### Matrix interactions

VPS forms the structural backbone of the extracellular matrix and interacts with

- [[RbmA]] → cell-cell adhesion
- [[RbmC]] → biofilm envelope stabilization
- [[Bap1]] → surface adhesion
- extracellular DNA (eDNA)
- other EPS components

Together these components generate the mature biofilm matrix.

---

#### Biological significance

- essential for mature biofilm formation in *V. cholerae*
- contributes to environmental persistence
- promotes host and environmental surface colonization
- supports resistance to physical and chemical stresses
- central determinant of biofilm architecture

#### Biosynthesis

VPS is synthesized through a canonical Wzx/Wzy-dependent pathway.

Assembly order

- VpsL initiates Und-PP-Glc
- VpsA/B/K generate ManNAcA precursor
- VpsJ converts ManNAcA → L-GulNAcA
- VpsH adds glycine modification
- VpsG acetylates repeat unit
- VpsI extends oligosaccharide
- VpsD completes repeat unit
- VpsE flips repeat across membrane
- VpsF polymerizes mature VPS

VpsD substrate flexibility generates both major and minor VPS variants.
