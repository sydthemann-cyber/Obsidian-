# VirB4 (TrbE)

### Summary

VirB4 (TrbE) is the ATPase responsible for assembly and function of the conjugative Type IV secretion system. It is a major evolutionary target of plasmid-dependent phages because loss-of-function mutations eliminate pilus production, preventing infection while reducing conjugation.

### Notes

- ATPase
- T4SS assembly
- pilus biogenesis
- DNA transfer
- common PRD1 resistance mutation
- truncation → loss of conjugation
- IS insertions frequently disrupt gene
- natural IncP plasmids often contain truncated variants
