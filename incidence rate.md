= # new cases / total person time at risk 

IR = # incident cases / T 

Also called _person-time incidence rate, hazard rate_. New cases per unit person-time. Units 1/time, range 0 to ∞. Works in open or closed populations. "Instantaneous risk".

incidence rate measures how fast new cases occur (over a time unit)
