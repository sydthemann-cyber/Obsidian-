"==a phenomenon where a cell or organism already infected by a virus—most commonly a bacterium harboring a temperate bacteriophage prophage—becomes resistant to secondary infections by the same or closely related viruses==. This protection stops incoming viral genomes from triggering lytic cycles, ensuring the survival of both the host cell and the resident prophage. [[1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6550527/), [2](https://www.archaealviruses.org/terms/superinfection_immunity.html), [3](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/superinfection)]"
