### summary
toxR is a master virulence regulator in v. cholerae. it controls expression of outer membrane proteins, toxT, and virulence genes, integrating environmental and host-derived cues.

### functions
- activates major virulence regulons (e.g., toxT)  
- regulates outer membrane porins and stress response genes  
- functions with ToxS as a membrane-bound transcription factor complex  
- connects environmental signals (pH, bile salts, temperature) to gene expression
